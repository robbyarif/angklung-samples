來自哪裡

Where does it come from?
1

Angklung 來自印尼西爪哇，是巽他族（Sunda）的傳統竹製樂器。

From West Java — a traditional bamboo instrument of the Sundanese people.
2

它在爪哇與峇里島一帶流傳了很久，不同地方有不同的調音與玩法。

Long used across Java and Bali, with different tunings and styles in each region.
3

2010 年，聯合國教科文組織（UNESCO）把它列入「人類非物質文化遺產代表作名錄」。

In 2010 UNESCO inscribed it on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

音階基礎

What is the musical basis?
1

傳統 Angklung 使用印尼自己的五聲音階（如 slendro、pelog），跟我們熟悉的 Do Re Mi 不太一樣。

Traditional angklung use Indonesian pentatonic scales (slendro, pelog) — not the familiar do-re-mi.
2

1938 年左右，萬隆的 Daeng Soetigna 把它改成 Do Re Mi 七聲音階，這樣就能演奏西洋歌曲和各國民謠。

Around 1938, Daeng Soetigna of Bandung adapted it to the diatonic scale, so any song could be played.
3

一支 Angklung 只發一個音，所以要很多人一起，每人負責自己的音才能合奏出旋律。

One angklung = one note. A melody needs a group, each person with their own note.

用來做什麼

What is it for?
1

最早與稻米有關：傳統上在祈求豐收、感謝稻米女神 Dewi Sri 的儀式中演奏。

Rooted in rice farming: played in ceremonies for a good harvest and for the rice goddess Dewi Sri.
2

也用在遊行、慶典與節日，讓大家一起搖、一起開心。

Also used in processions, festivals and celebrations.
3

現在更是學校音樂教育和合奏表演的好工具，因為它很容易上手、也很需要合作。

Today it is a popular tool in school music education and group performances — easy to start, built on teamwork.

用什麼做

What is it made of?
1

竹子是主角。常使用黑竹（awi wulung）等質地好的竹種，要乾燥後才會音色穩定。

Bamboo is the star — often black bamboo (awi wulung), dried well so the tone stays stable.
2

每支由竹框和兩到三根竹管組成，竹管用藤條或繩子鬆鬆地固定在框上。

A frame plus two or three tubes, loosely tied in place with rattan or cord.
3

竹管被削出開口，再一點一點削磨調音——長度和厚薄決定音高。

Each tube is cut with a slit and shaved little by little to tune it — length and thickness set the pitch.

聲音的邏輯

The logic of the sound
1

搖動時，竹管在框裡來回撞擊，每撞一下就響一聲，連起來就是顫動的音。

Shaking makes the tubes knock back and forth in the frame — each knock rings, and together they shimmer.
2

每支有兩根管子，音高相差一個八度，所以聲音飽滿、不單薄。

The two tubes sit one octave apart, which gives a full, rich tone.
3

管子越長，音越低；越短，音越高。這就是旁邊那排竹管高低不同的原因！

Longer tube = lower note; shorter tube = higher note.

怎麼搖

How to play
1

一手握住框架，另一手保持輕鬆，手腕左右輕搖。

Hold the frame with one hand and shake gently from the wrist.
2

顫音（kurulung）：連續搖動，聲音會延長、很柔和。

Kurulung (tremolo): keep shaking for a long, shimmering note.
3

短音（centok）：用手指輕輕彈一下，只發出一個短短的音。

Centok (short note): a quick flick gives one short sound.
4

螢幕上也一樣：點一下是短音，按住不放是顫音。

On screen too: tap for a short note, hold for tremolo.
